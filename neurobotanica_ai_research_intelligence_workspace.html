Standard Operating Protocol

Reproducible research workflow.

A structured six-stage methodology from taxonomic disambiguation to a defensible novelty statement suitable for grant proposals and systematic scoping.

STAGE 01Define Scope & Target

Select one botanical taxon, therapeutic axis, plant organ/extract scope, and primary assay endpoints.

STAGE 02Resolve GBIF Taxonomy

Anchor searches on the accepted binomial and taxonomic synonyms to prevent missed legacy literature.

STAGE 03Multi-Source Retrieval

Execute structured Boolean queries across Europe PMC, OpenAlex, and Semantic Scholar with full query logging.

STAGE 04Deduplicate & Filter

Merge cross-database hits via DOI, PMID, and normalized title keys; enforce species mention in title or abstract.

STAGE 05Extract Evidence Matrix

Tag plant part, extraction solvent, biological model, material class (crude vs. isolate), and assay readout.

STAGE 06Human Verification

Inspect source abstracts/full texts, confirm or reject automated tags, and log manual novelty overrides.

Methodological Reporting Standard Always report: “No matching records were retrieved from Europe PMC, OpenAlex, or Semantic Scholar using the logged search strings on [date].” Never claim a species has “never been studied.”
